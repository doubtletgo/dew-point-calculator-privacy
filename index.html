Privacy Policy for Dew Point Calculator

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dew Point Calculator (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is provided as a utility for weather-related calculations and code references.

The App is designed to perform its calculations on your device. The developer does not intentionally collect, receive, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location through the App.
